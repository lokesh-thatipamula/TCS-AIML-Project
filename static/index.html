Standardized Insurance Risk Data Schema

JSON Schema specifications and normalization dictionary without Personal Identifiable Information (PII)

Schema Highlights

  • customer_id: Synthetic anonymized identifier (e.g. CUST-AUTO-101).
  • demographics: Age, occupation category, territory risk tier (Low to Very High), credit score tier.
  • policy_details: Policy type (Auto, Property, Health, Life), coverage amount, deductible, premium.
  • claim_history: Total lifetime claims, 36-month claim volume, incurred indemnity amounts, at-fault fault ratios, itemized incidents.
  • risk_factors: Telematics score (Auto), property age/roof/flood/wildfire (Property), smoker status/BMI/chronic conditions (Health/Life).
  • PII Guardrail: Automated preprocessor sanitization stripping names, phone numbers, emails, SSNs.
